  License: Apache License 2.0 - https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0
  PDFium: Copyright (c) The PDFium Authors. https://pdfium.googlesource.com/pdfium/

PDFsharp (PDFsharp-wpf, https://docs.pdfsharp.net/)
  Writing annotations into PDF pages.
  License: MIT
  Copyright (c) PDFsharp Team

CommunityToolkit.Mvvm (https://github.com/CommunityToolkit/dotnet)
  License: MIT
  Copyright (c) .NET Foundation and Contributors

The Full edition of the installer also contains the .NET runtime
(dotnet-LICENSE.txt and dotnet-THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt next to the program).

Microsoft.Web.WebView2 (https://www.nuget.org/packages/Microsoft.Web.WebView2)
  Lays out EPUB books into pages, through the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime that is part of Windows.
  License: Microsoft software license terms (BSD-style, see the package)
